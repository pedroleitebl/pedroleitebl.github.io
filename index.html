About

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Economics at the Universidade Federal de Juiz de Fora (UFJF), affiliated with IPEA and advised by Professor Rodolfo Freguglia. My research focuses on technology adoption and labor market dynamics using large-scale Brazilian administrative data, including RAIS, TIC Empresas, and PNAD. I currently work as a research assistant for Mayara Felix (Yale University) and Rodimiro Rodrigo (George Washington University), and I hold a Master's degree in Applied Economics and a degree in Production Engineering.
